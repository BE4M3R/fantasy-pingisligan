## Participation

Participation in The Weekly Smash is free and automatic. No additional registration is required.

To participate in a Gameweek, a manager must have a valid Fantasy team of six players locked in before that Gameweek's deadline, in accordance with the existing Fantasy squad rules.

Each Gameweek is a separate competition. A manager may win more than one Gameweek.

Managers under 18 are intended to be eligible, subject to applicable legal requirements. If a winner is under 18, confirmation from a parent or legal guardian is required before the prize is awarded. Guardian information is collected only when necessary for prize fulfilment, not from every participant at registration. The legal and privacy requirements for participation by minors remain subject to review before publication.

## How the winner is selected

Only one prize is awarded per Gameweek. Eligible teams are compared in this order:

1. **Highest total Gameweek points.** The existing net Fantasy Gameweek score applies, including captain multipliers, chips, automatic substitutions and transfer penalties.
2. **Highest raw captain score.** If teams are tied on total Gameweek points, compare their effective captains' raw Fantasy points before any captain multiplier or chip-related boost. Triple Captain provides no additional tie-breaking advantage. The existing automatic captain substitution rules apply.
3. **Documented random draw.** If teams remain tied after the captain comparison, one winner is selected through a fair, documented random draw among those remaining tied.

The results, eligibility and any outstanding fixtures must be verified, and the winner must be manually confirmed before publication. A results refresh alone does not confirm a winner. Later result corrections may need to be considered during verification.

The captain comparison is a prize tie-breaker only. It does not change Fantasy Gameweek points or the scoring system.

The specific prize is displayed on the corresponding competition page. Weekly prizes are table tennis products with an approximate retail value of SEK 500, provided by Wood House AB, the sponsoring partner representing Yasaka, Donic and Nittaku.

## Winner announcement & claiming

The confirmed winning Fantasy team is announced on Pingisligan Fantasy after verification. Public announcements use the Fantasy team name recorded in the locked Gameweek snapshot, rather than the manager's personal details.

Pingisligan Fantasy contacts the winner at their registered email address. The winner has **five calendar days from the notification email being sent** to respond and claim the prize.

The winner may also initiate contact through [our official contact page](/about), [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/pingisliganfantasy/) or [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=61594357529758). Identity must be verified before prize fulfilment.

If the winner does not respond within five calendar days, declines the prize or is found ineligible, the prize is offered to the next eligible manager according to the competition ranking and the same tie-breaking rules. A replacement winner receives a new five-calendar-day claiming period, starting when their notification email is sent.

## Prize delivery

Wood House AB supplies the prize and arranges and pays for prize shipping.

Pingisligan Fantasy requests the winner's name and necessary delivery information. Only the details necessary to fulfil the prize are forwarded to Wood House AB, solely for that purpose.

Personal delivery information must never be displayed publicly. For a winner under 18, the required parent or legal guardian confirmation must be obtained before the prize is awarded; necessary guardian and delivery information is collected only when needed for fulfilment.

## Taxation

Any applicable tax treatment is determined under Swedish law and the legal classification of the competition. Weekly prizes are not automatically tax-free.

The winner is responsible for any applicable personal income tax. This does not remove any reporting or other obligations that apply to Pingisligan Fantasy as organizer or to the prize provider.

The final random tie-break may affect the competition's legal classification and taxation. Its application must be reviewed before these terms are approved or real prizes are awarded.

See [Skatteverket's official guidance on winnings from games and competitions](https://www.skatteverket.se/privat/skatter/arbeteochinkomst/inkomster/vinsterispelochtavlingar.4.7459477810df5bccdd4800015166.html).
