## Participation

Participation is free and automatic. Enter by having a valid Fantasy squad of six players locked in before the Gameweek deadline.

Each Gameweek is a separate competition, and a manager may win more than once.

## Winner selection

Each winner is decided by criteria below, in order:

1. Highest total Gameweek points.
2. Highest captain score in that Gameweek, before captain multipliers. Triple Captain provides no extra advantage.
3. Highest position in the season standings after this Gameweek, before the next Gameweek begins.
4. Random draw if teams are still tied.

## Winner announcement & claiming

Winners are announced on this website and our social media channels.

Pingisligan Fantasy contacts the winner at their registered email address to request a prize delivery address. The winner has **five days from the email being sent** to respond and claim the prize. Otherwise we will reach out to second place to receive prize.

The delivery address and only necessary information are forwarded to Wood House AB, which supplies the prize and arranges shipping. Personal delivery information is not published.

## Taxation

Qualifying non-cash competition prizes worth up to **SEK 1,800 are normally tax-free in 2026** under Swedish rules. Our weekly Gameweek prizes are intended to fall within this exemption. The winner is responsible for any tax that applies.

See [Skatteverket's guidance on competition prizes](https://www.skatteverket.se/privat/skatter/arbeteochinkomst/inkomster/vinsterispelochtavlingar.4.7459477810df5bccdd4800015166.html).
