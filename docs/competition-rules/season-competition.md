## Participation

Participation in the Season Competition is free and automatic. No separate registration is required.

A manager qualifies by having a valid Fantasy team of six players locked in for at least one Gameweek during the applicable season, in accordance with the existing Fantasy squad rules.

Managers under 18 are intended to be eligible, subject to applicable legal requirements. If a winner is under 18, confirmation from a parent or legal guardian is required before the prize is awarded. Necessary guardian information is collected only when needed for prize fulfilment, not from all participants at registration. The legal and privacy requirements for participation by minors remain subject to review before publication.

## How the winner is selected

Only one season prize is awarded. Eligible managers are compared in this order:

1. **Highest accumulated season points.** The existing Fantasy season scoring applies, including applicable captain multipliers, chips, substitutions and transfer deductions.
2. **Highest accumulated raw captain points.** If managers are tied on total season points, compare the sum of their effective captains' raw Fantasy points across the applicable season, excluding captain multipliers and chip-related boosts. The existing automatic captain substitution rules apply to each Gameweek.
3. **Documented random draw.** If managers remain tied, one winner is selected through a fair, documented random draw among those remaining tied.

The winner must be manually verified and confirmed after the season's results, including any outstanding fixtures and relevant corrections, are settled. The captain comparison is a prize tie-breaker only and does not change Fantasy scores.

## Winner announcement & claiming

The confirmed Season Competition winner is announced on Pingisligan Fantasy after verification, using their Fantasy team name. Personal contact and delivery details are not published.

Pingisligan Fantasy contacts the winner at their registered email address. The winner has **ten calendar days from the notification email being sent** to respond, claim the prize and select their preferred prize alternative.

The winner chooses **one** of these alternatives:

- **Table tennis table:** a Yasaka Progress Roller Competition table, including net and posts, with an approximate retail value of SEK 16,000. It may result in taxable income, and the winner is responsible for any applicable personal income tax.
- **Yasaka gift card:** a gift card worth SEK 1,800. It is intended as a potentially qualifying non-cash alternative. Its redemption conditions, including the assumption that it cannot be redeemed for cash, must be confirmed with Wood House AB before publication and prize fulfilment. Tax exemption is not guaranteed.

Only one alternative may be selected. There is no cash prize option.

The winner may also initiate contact through [our official contact page](/about), [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/pingisliganfantasy/) or [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=61594357529758), subject to identity verification before fulfilment.

If the winner does not respond within ten calendar days, declines the prize or is ineligible, the prize is offered to the next eligible manager under the same ranking and tie-breaking rules. A replacement winner receives a new ten-calendar-day period from the sending of their notification email to claim and select their prize.

## Prize delivery

Wood House AB supplies the selected prize and arranges and pays for delivery.

Pingisligan Fantasy collects the winner's name and necessary delivery information and shares only the details required by Wood House AB, solely for prize fulfilment. Personal delivery information must never be displayed publicly.

For a winner under 18, appropriate confirmation from a parent or legal guardian is required before the prize is awarded, subject to legal review. Necessary guardian and delivery information is collected only when needed for fulfilment.

## Taxation

Tax treatment depends on Swedish law and the legal classification of the competition. The final random tie-break and its effect on that classification must be reviewed before these terms are approved or real prizes are awarded.

The table tennis table has an approximate retail value of SEK 16,000 and may result in taxable income.

The SEK 1,800 gift card alternative may qualify for the non-cash competition prize exemption only if the applicable conditions are met. Skatteverket lists SEK 1,800 as the relevant threshold for qualifying competition prizes in the **2026 tax year**. This is not a permanent threshold for future years; the rules and threshold applicable to the actual prize-award year must be checked.

Cash redeemability, competition classification and the other applicable conditions must be verified. Neither the gift card's value nor its description as a non-cash alternative guarantees tax exemption.

The winner is responsible for any applicable personal income tax. This does not remove any reporting or other obligations of Pingisligan Fantasy as organizer or of the prize provider.

See [Skatteverket's official guidance on winnings from games and competitions](https://www.skatteverket.se/privat/skatter/arbeteochinkomst/inkomster/vinsterispelochtavlingar.4.7459477810df5bccdd4800015166.html).
